# List My Car Hire Company: A UK Airport Operator's Guide to Joining Camel Global

If you run a car hire business serving Heathrow, Gatwick, Manchester, Birmingham, Edinburgh, Bristol, Luton or Stansted, you already know the daily grind of chasing bookings, managing fleet downtime and competing for visibility against operators with bigger marketing budgets. Camel Global is a car rental marketplace for companies that gives independent and regional operators a straightforward way to list their fleet, pick up qualified bookings, and run a meet & greet style service without disrupting how you already work. This guide walks through exactly how the process works, what to expect, and how it fits alongside the systems you already have in place.

## Why UK Airport Operators Are Looking Beyond the Big OTAs

Most UK car hire companies already have listings on the major booking sites. That's not a problem - Camel Global isn't asking you to replace anything. The issue many operators face is that those channels put you in a crowded queue where price is often the only lever you can pull. There's little room to bid on the bookings that suit your fleet, your depot location, or your availability on a given day. A car rental partner program built around meet & greet delivery works differently: it's closer to how ride-hailing apps connect drivers with passengers, giving you the chance to respond directly to requests rather than sit passively in a list.

## How the Camel Global Partner Program Works

At its core, Camel Global operates as a supplier portal where car hire companies list their available vehicles and respond to customer requests as they come in. Customers post what they need - vehicle type, dates, pickup location near a UK airport - and partners bid on the bookings they want to take. This competitive bidding model means you're not forced to accept every request at a fixed rate; you choose which jobs fit your fleet and schedule.

The meet & greet element is central to how bookings are delivered. Rather than customers queuing at a rental desk, your team meets them directly, in a model similar to how private hire drivers meet passengers. This suits UK airport car hire particularly well, since terminal car parks and short-stay bays are expensive and inconvenient for both operators and customers.

## What You Get as a Partner

Camel Global is a live platform with real bookings processed through Stripe, so payments are handled securely without you needing to build your own payment infrastructure. The platform supports multiple currencies and six languages, which matters for UK airports that see a steady flow of international travellers arriving at Heathrow, Gatwick and Manchester who may prefer to book in their own language and currency before they land.

Joining is free, with no monthly fees to list your fleet. There's no subscription cost sitting on your books whether or not you're winning bookings that week. As a UK operator listing early, you're also positioned as a founding partner in your area - meaning you get established on the platform before it becomes more competitive locally, giving you first access to the bookings coming through in your region.

## Setup: What Actually Happens When You Apply

One of the most common objections we hear is that joining a new platform means hours of admin, technical setup, or learning a complicated dashboard. That's not how this works. Applying takes around five minutes. You'll need basic details about your business - company name, VAT/Tax number, contact details, and information about your fleet, such as vehicle types, availability and your operating base near the airport you serve. There's no need for technical knowledge or integration work with your existing booking software. The supplier portal is built to sit alongside whatever systems you already use for fleet management, driver scheduling or invoicing.

As the partner, you remain the supplier of record - you issue the VAT invoice to the customer and retain full control over your fleet, your drivers and your pricing decisions on each bid.

## Addressing the Quality Question

A fair concern for any operator is whether bookings from a new channel will actually convert, or whether you'll spend time bidding on requests that go nowhere. Because customers submit specific requests with real dates and locations, and payments run through Stripe, the process is built around genuine transactions rather than speculative browsing. You retain control by bidding only on the jobs that match your availability and vehicle types, so you're not committing fleet capacity to uncertain demand.

## Will Smaller and Regional Operators Get Seen?

If you're a smaller operator based near a regional airport rather than one of the major London hubs, visibility is a legitimate concern. Camel Global's structure is built around location-based requests - customers searching for pickup near your specific airport will see partners operating in that area, not just the largest national names. Being an early, founding partner in your region means you're establishing your presence on the platform before more operators join locally, which matters if you're trying to get more car rental bookings without significantly increasing your marketing spend.

## How This Fits Alongside Your Existing Business

Nothing about becoming a car hire partner with Camel Global requires you to change your current operations, drop existing OTA listings, or restructure your fleet. It's designed to run in parallel - an additional channel for bookings that complements your website, your existing agreements, and your walk-up or repeat-customer business. Your drivers, your vehicles, your depot and your customer relationships stay exactly as they are; the platform simply adds another route through which UK travellers can find and book you.

## Getting Started

If you're ready to list your car hire company, the application takes about five minutes and there's no cost to join. Set up your profile, add your available vehicles, and start bidding on requests from travellers arriving at UK airports near you. Joining early as a founding partner means you're positioned to capture bookings in your area as demand grows on the platform - with no monthly fees and no obligation beyond the bookings you choose to take.
