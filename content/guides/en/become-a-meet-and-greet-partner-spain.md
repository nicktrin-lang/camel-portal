# How to Become a Meet & Greet Car Hire Partner in Spain

If you run a car hire business in Spain, Camel Global sends you ready-to-fulfil booking requests from travellers who want a meet & greet handover. You bid, you win, you deliver.

- **Real demand** — customers come to us looking for local partners
- **You set your price** — bid what works for your business
- **Paid in your currency** — settlement in EUR, no FX surprises
- **No lock-in** — bid on the jobs you want

## How it works

1. Sign up and tell us your base, fleet and service radius
2. Get matched to nearby requests automatically
3. Submit a bid; if the customer accepts, it's yours
4. Meet the customer, hand over the car, get paid at month-end

## What you need

A registered car hire business, a VAT/tax number, at least one vehicle, and a driver to handle collection and drop-off.
